About RealEstate Hub

RealEstate Hub was established to simplify the property buying, renting, and investment
process across Pakistan's major cities. We work with a network of verified developers
including Bahria Town Developers, DHA City Developments, Emaar Pakistan, Fazaia Housing
Scheme, and Lake City Developers. Our listings span residential houses, apartments,
commercial units, and investment plots across Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi.
Every property listed on our platform goes through a verification process before it
appears to clients, covering ownership documentation, developer NOC status where
applicable, and physical site condition checks.

Our Services

We offer end-to-end support across four main service lines. First, property search and
recommendation, where our consultants match client requirements — budget, location,
property type, and purpose — against our verified inventory. Second, site visit
coordination, which is provided free of charge regardless of whether a booking is made.
Third, legal and documentation support, including registry assistance, transfer
paperwork, and dispute-free title verification. Fourth, post-sale support, covering
handover coordination and ongoing client queries after a transaction closes.

Payment Plans and Financing

Most under-construction and newly launched properties on our platform offer flexible
installment plans, typically ranging from 20% to 50% down payment with the remaining
balance spread across 12 to 48 months depending on the developer's policy. Ready-to-move
properties, particularly resale units, often require full payment at the time of
transfer, though this varies by seller. Clients interested in bank financing can request
guidance through our partner bank relationships; final loan approval remains subject to
each bank's independent underwriting policy, and RealEstate Hub does not guarantee loan
approval outcomes. Late installment payments may be subject to penalties as defined in
each property's individual payment plan documentation, and clients should review these
terms carefully before committing to a purchase.

Rental Policy

Rental agreements arranged through RealEstate Hub are typically structured for an
eleven-month term, renewable by mutual agreement between landlord and tenant. Security
deposits are generally equivalent to two months' rent, though this can vary by property
and landlord preference. Furnished and semi-furnished rental options are available on a
subset of listings and are clearly indicated in each property's description. Tenants are
responsible for utility costs unless otherwise stated in the rental agreement, and any
maintenance requests during the tenancy period should be routed through our post-sale
support line.

Investment Guidance

For clients exploring real estate as an investment vehicle, we provide historical
appreciation and rental yield data where available, sourced from our own transaction
records and publicly available market reports. It is important to note that RealEstate
Hub does not guarantee future appreciation, rental yield, or investment returns of any
kind; all figures shared with clients are historical and illustrative only, not a
promise of future performance. Areas such as Bahria Town extensions and newer DHA phases
have shown strong historical appreciation trends, but individual investment outcomes
depend on market conditions, property-specific factors, and timing that are outside our
control.

Commercial Property Considerations

Clients purchasing or renting commercial units should be aware that additional
documentation is typically required, including business registration details and a
valid NTN (National Tax Number) for the purchasing entity. Commercial properties are
often subject to different zoning and usage regulations depending on the specific
development authority governing the area, and our consultants can provide zoning
information available at the time of inquiry. Some commercial listings require full
payment prior to possession rather than offering installment plans, and this is
specified individually per listing.

Site Visits and Booking Process

Site visits can be scheduled seven days a week between 10:00 AM and 7:00 PM, based on
client availability, and there is no charge for this service. Families are encouraged to
attend visits together so that all relevant decision-makers can evaluate a property
directly. No booking amount or deposit is required simply to view a property; a booking
is only initiated once a client has made a decision to proceed. If a client does not
find a suitable property during an initial visit, our consultants will continue
proposing alternative options that better match stated requirements at no additional
cost.

Legal and Title Verification

All properties listed with RealEstate Hub are verified for clear title prior to being
made available to clients, and supporting documentation is made available for review
during the site visit process. In the rare event that a title dispute or documentation
concern arises after listing, our legal support team is available to guide clients
through the appropriate resolution steps. RealEstate Hub's legal advisory support during
the registry and transfer process is provided at no additional charge to the client.

Frequently Escalated Situations

Certain client situations are routed directly to a human consultant rather than being
handled purely through automated channels. These include requests for price negotiation
below the listed price, detailed financial projections for large commercial or
investment transactions, complaints regarding a prior interaction with the company, and
any legal or contractual matter beyond standard registry guidance. Clients are always
informed clearly when their inquiry is being escalated, along with an expected timeline
for a human consultant to follow up.

Contact and Operating Hours

RealEstate Hub's consultant line operates daily from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM across all
covered cities. Email inquiries are typically answered within one business day. Clients
with an active booking or ongoing transaction are assigned a dedicated point of contact
for the duration of their engagement with the company.
